**Sortly** is a well-built inventory app — for businesses. Over the years it has moved upmarket toward teams, warehouses, and field service, and the home user has been left with a thin free tier and pricing that assumes a company card.

If you just want to know what is in your closets, garage, and storage unit, here is the best **Sortly alternative** and why it fits home use better.

---

## Where Sortly Pinches for Home Users

* **100-item free limit.** One user, 100 unique items. A single garage blows past that.
* **Export is paid.** CSV and PDF export — the thing you need for an insurance claim — is not on the free plan.
* **Subscription pricing.** Paid plans start around $49/month, scaled for business budgets.
* **Cloud-first.** Your item list lives on Sortly's servers behind an account.
* **No on-device AI.** You type item names yourself.

None of that is wrong for a business. It is a poor fit for cataloging your own home.
