**ToteScan** made bin-scanning mainstream: stick a numbered label on a tote, scan it, and see a list of what is inside. The idea is great. The catch is the business model — the app only works with **their** label packs, at roughly $12–$16 a pack, and if you run out mid-project you are waiting on shipping.

If you like the scan-a-bin concept but not the sticker lock-in, here is the best **ToteScan alternative** and how it differs.

---

## The Problem With Proprietary Labels

* **Recurring cost.** Every new bin needs another purchased label. Organize a whole basement and it adds up.
* **Supply risk.** Run out on moving day and the system stops until a delivery arrives.
* **Lock-in.** The labels only mean anything inside that one app. Lose access, lose the system.
* **No printer option.** You cannot just print more or write one by hand.

## When ToteScan Still Makes Sense

If you specifically want rugged, pre-printed, uniform numbered labels and you do not mind buying them, ToteScan's physical labels are well made. The switch is worth it when you are labeling a lot of containers, want photo-first entry, or do not want your inventory on someone else's server.
